# Phase 3: display, msm DRM drives the eDP panel (2026-11-03)

Attempt `2026-11-03-4`. Image `ab87e567a0eb05bd`, kernel `#5` (2026-11-03 2:09 PM ET), commit `072f347`. Sources: the
netlog `dumps/firstboot/2026-11-03-netlog.txt` and the AP console log `dumps/firstboot/2026-11-03-ap.log` (both
gitignored), plus the owner looking at the panel.

## Why

Every earlier boot went dark at about 33 s: `VREG_EDP_3P3: disabling` (attempt 2026-11-03-3,
[wifi-2026-11-03.md](wifi-2026-11-03.md)). The mica DTS already described the mainline display path (`&mdss`,
`&mdss_dp3` with an `edp-panel` on its aux-bus, `&mdss_dp3_phy`, `vreg_edp_3p3`, `pwm-backlight`), matching the stock
`sde_edp` (`entry01-overlay.dts` fragment 11). But every driver on it was `=m` in defconfig at `a804c57`, and the
initramfs loads no modules. Only simpledrm drove the screen, nothing claimed the panel supply, and the regulator core
turned it off as unused.

## Changes (commit `072f347`)

- `kernel/mica-display.config`: `DRM_MSM`, `PHY_QCOM_EDP`, `CLK_X1E80100_DISPCC`, `DRM_PANEL_EDP`,
  `BACKLIGHT_CLASS_DEVICE`, `BACKLIGHT_PWM` and `LEDS_QCOM_LPG` set `=y`, plus the dependency chain: `QCOM_LLCC` and
  `QCOM_OCMEM` (`DRM_MSM=y` needs them `=y` or `=n`), and `LEDS_CLASS_MULTICOLOR` and `QCOM_PBS` for the LPG.
- Command line: `msm.skip_gpu=1`. Otherwise the GPU would join the MDSS component match (`msm_drv.c:1076-1086`) and
  wait on gpucc, whose SMMU `3da0000.iommu` is still deferred.
- DTS: the backlight's `edp_bl_en` pin state moves from TLMM 74 (left over from the 9345; stock muxes it to QUP SE18)
  to PMC8380-3 GPIO 4, the stock `qcom,edp-backlight-en-gpio`. The PWM pin takes the stock `edp_pwm_active` config.
- `/init`: DRM connectors, the backlight, the panel supplies after uptime 40 s, the panel identity from the EDID (no
  serials; `check.sh` test 16), display dmesg, and a banner on `tty1`.

## Result: pass

The panel stayed lit past 40 s and showed the `GBOOK: display check` banner (the owner saw it).

- **msm binds early:**
  - `[drm] Initialized msm 1.13.0 for ae01000.display-controller on minor 1` at 1.154 s.
  - `dpu hardware revision:0x90020000`.
  - All three DP controllers bound (`ae90000`, `ae98000`, `aea0000`).
  - `fb0: msmdrmfb frame buffer device` at 1.595 s, taking over from simpledrm's `fb0` (0.453 s).
  - `no GPU device was found`: expected with `msm.skip_gpu=1`.
- **Connectors:** `card1-eDP-1 status=connected enabled=enabled modes=2560x1600`. `card1-DP-1` and `card1-DP-2` are
  disconnected (nothing on Type-C), and `card1-Writeback-1` is there too.
- **Panel supply:** `VREG_EDP_3P3 state=enabled users=1` and `VBL9 state=enabled users=1` after 40 s. No
  `VREG_EDP_3P3: disabling` line this boot.
- **Backlight:** `backlight max=4095 actual=2048 power=0`, a 12-bit PWM at 50 %. Stock caps the level at `0x190`
  (fragment 11) and `0x200` (fragment 77); mainline uses the LPG's full range.
- **Panel:** `Unknown panel LGD 0x0804, using conservative timings`. That's LG Display, product `0x0804`, not in
  `edp_panels[]` at `a804c57`. The `WARN_ON` splat at `panel-edp.c:829` is the driver's deliberate "add a table
  entry" signal (comment at `:824-828`), not a fault. Conservative timings only lengthen the power sequencing delays.
- **Deferred at the end:** only `arm-scmi` (mailbox `17430000`). `3da0000.iommu` was listed at the first check and
  not at the last.

## Not yet captured

- `GBOOK: panel-id=card1-eDP-1 none`: sysfs reports the `edid` attribute's size as 0, so `[ -s ]` skipped a readable
  file. Fixed in commit `2d2f2b5` (test `-r`; `none` on a short read). The next image will print the EDID name and
  text descriptors.
- The dropbox readback (`attempt.sh readback`) didn't run because adb was down after the stock boot (Wireless
  debugging). Netlog has the whole log, so nothing is missing.
