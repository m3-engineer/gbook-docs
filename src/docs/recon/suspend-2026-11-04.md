# Suspend (s2idle) on mica, 2026-11-04

ADR 0009's revisit trigger: one s2idle round trip per boot, from `/init` with `gbook.suspend=1` on the command line
(commits `cf49174`, `c3fa7e7`). The wake is the cros-ec-rtc alarm, 20 s after entry. Six cold boots, attempts
`2026-11-04-5` to `-10` (`dumps/firstboot/attempts.tsv`). Sources: the AP and EC SuzyQ console logs, and stock's
`SYSTEM_LAST_KMSG` (ramoops) via `scripts/attempt.sh readback`. Times are kernel uptime unless marked EC.

## Result

**Suspend works; resume resets the SoC every time.** Entry, the device suspend and the wake all work. The resume dies
in the PCIe noirq resume, and the EC then power-cycles the AP. No netlog arrives from these boots.

| Attempt | Kernel, DT extra | Where the resume died |
|---|---|---|
| -5 | #21 | Unseen: the console sleeps during suspend (`Suspending console(s)`) |
| -6 | #21, `no_console_suspend` | `gcc_pcie_6a_gdsc status stuck at 'off'` (WARNING `gdsc.c:192`, `genpd_resume_noirq`), then silence |
| -7 | #21, `dts/diag/no-pcie6a.dtsi` | `gcc_pcie_4_gdsc status stuck at 'off'`, then silence |
| -8 | #22 (patch 0008) | No GDSC warning; nothing printed before the reset |
| -9 | #23 (0008 + `pm_print_times`) | `pci 0006:01:00.0` (NVMe, no driver) `pci_pm_resume_noirq` never returned; last line `qcom-pcie 1c08000` (PCIe 4) `genpd_resume_noirq` called |
| -10 | #23, no PCIe 6a | `qcom-pcie 1c08000` (PCIe 4) `genpd_resume_noirq` called at 144.093 s, never returned; every other noirq resume returned. Before this boot the Googlebook went to `G3` 18 s after a first power-on (EC 451.46 s), cause not known, and was powered on again |

## What the logs show

- **The wake path works.** In -5 the EC logged `Handle sleep: 1` at EC 2359.68 s and the RTC host event
  (`event set 0x02000000`) at EC 2379.80 s, 20.1 s later. Then `AP_RST_L transitions not expected` came 0.76 s later
  (-8: 0.70 s), and `MKBP: The AP is failing to respond`. The EC ran a host reboot (`Reset cause: hard`), and the
  firmware fell through to stock.
- **The PCIe GDSCs.** At a804c57, `gcc-x1e80100.c` gives the PCIe GDSCs `PWRSTS_OFF_ON`, so `gdsc_disable()`
  really switches them off at suspend (`gdsc.c:369-380`). On resume they stayed off (-6, -7). sc8280xp's PCIe GDSCs use
  `PWRSTS_RET_ON`, which leaves them on and lets the hardware retain them.
  - Upstream: "clk: qcom: gcc-x1e80100: Do not turn off PCIe GDSCs during gdsc_disable()", Krishna Chaitanya
    Chundru (Qualcomm), patch 6/7 of a series, LKML 2026-01-02, https://lkml.rescloud.iu.edu/2601.0/00459.html,
    Reviewed-by Bryan O'Donoghue. It is not in a804c57.
  - It is now local patch `kernel/patches/0008` (ADR 0007). With it, the GDSC warnings are gone (-8, -9, -10).
- **The resume still dies in the PCIe controllers.** With `PM_SLEEP_DEBUG` (`kernel/mica-pmdebug.config`) and
  `pm_print_times`:
  - **-9:** PCIe 6a's controller resumed (`genpd_resume_noirq` 526 µs) and so did its root port. The NVMe endpoint's
    `pci_pm_resume_noirq` then never returned, while other devices went on resuming for about 100 ms.
  - **-10:** with 6a disabled, PCIe 4's own controller resume (`qcom-pcie 1c08000`) never returned.
  - In both boots, PCIe 4's `genpd_suspend_noirq` took 3.07-3.29 s on the way in.
  - Ramoops ends on the same line as the console (-6, -9), so the reset leaves nothing more.
- **Two different failures (`pm_print_times` timing and the source).** `qcom_pcie_suspend_noirq()`
  (`pcie-qcom.c:2340` at a804c57) calls `dw_pcie_suspend_noirq()` (`pcie-designware-host.c:1225`). That either sends
  PME_Turn_Off, waits for L2 (polled in 1 ms steps) and shuts the host down, or returns at once and keeps the link,
  depending on `pci_host_common_d3cold_possible()` (`pci-host-common.c:269-325`).
  - **PCIe 6a kept its link.** Its `genpd_suspend_noirq` took 167 µs (-9) and its resume 526 µs: too short for
    either the L2 wait or a host re-init with link training. So the NVMe endpoint's config access went over a link
    that was never shut down, and still hung.
  - **PCIe 4 shut its link down.** Its `genpd_suspend_noirq` took 3.07-3.29 s (unexplained: the L2 poll times out
    at 10 ms), and on resume its host re-init (`qcom_pcie_resume_noirq`, `pcie-qcom.c:2398`) never returned.
  - **A lead, unverified.** Patch 0008 and the upstream series change only the `gcc_pcie_N_gdsc` entries. The PCIe
    PHY GDSCs (`gcc_pcie_3/4/5/6_phy_gdsc`) are still `PWRSTS_OFF_ON` (`gcc-x1e80100.c:6595-6663`), and
    `pcie6a_phy` has `power-domains = <&gcc GCC_PCIE_6_PHY_GDSC>` (`hamoa.dtsi:3546`). So genpd collapses the PHY's
    domain at suspend. The USB PHY GDSCs in the same file are already `PWRSTS_RET_ON` (`gcc-x1e80100.c:6775-6867`).
    Upstream changed those because "the QMP combo PHYs lose their configuration on machine suspend"
    ("clk: qcom: gcc-x1e80100: Fix USB 0 and 1 PHY GDSC pwrsts flags", patchwork comment 25978987). A PCIe PHY that
    lost its configuration would explain a dead link on 6a. That is an inference; the next boot can test it.
  - **Inference, not verified:** an access to a PCIe block that is not powered, or behind a dead link, hangs the bus.
    On Qualcomm that ends in a reset with no kernel message.
- **Other observations.**
  - The touchscreen bus times out on the way into suspend in every boot (`geni_i2c a80000.i2c: Timeout
    cancel_m_cmd`); this is the parked touchscreen fault and not fatal.
  - `mem_sleep` offers `s2idle [deep]`, so PSCI `SYSTEM_SUSPEND` is available too (inference from the kernel listing
    "deep"). It is untested.
  - SCMI was clean on every boot (0.1 ms).
  - Idle power stays unmeasured: on the bench the SuzyQ powers the Googlebook, so the battery's current is charge
    current.

2. Find the hanging step in PCIe 4's re-init: dyndbg for `pcie-qcom.c` and `pcie-designware-host.c` on one suspend

3. Try `deep` (`mem_sleep=deep`). It takes a different firmware path, and `qcom_pcie` then keeps the CPU-PCIe

4. Keep ADR 0009 as it is. Suspend fails before any idle state matters, so cpuidle is not the blocker yet (inference).

## Update: the first complete round trip, with patch 0009 (2026-11-04, 5:01 AM ET)

Attempt `2026-11-04-11`: kernel `#24` with patch 0008 and local patch 0009, which moves the four
`gcc_pcie_N_phy_gdsc` to `PWRSTS_RET_ON`. Board DTS, both PCIe links on. **The resume completed.** Source: netlog
`2026-11-04-netlog.txt#6`.

- **Timing:**
  - `PM: suspend entry (s2idle)` at 120.03 s;
  - suspend-to-idle from 126.24 s to 140.70 s (the RTC alarm);
  - noirq resume 883 ms, early resume 594 ms, resume 4855 ms (much of it console output from `pm_print_times`);
  - `PM: suspend exit` at 147.72 s.
- **Counters:** `rc=0`. `/sys/power/suspend_stats`: `success=1`, every failure counter 0.
- **After the resume:**
  - cpufreq: 3 policies, with 0 SCMI timeouts since the suspend.
  - Wi-Fi relinked in 8 s, same address, now on 5720 MHz (earlier boots joined on 6 GHz).
  - `/init` ran to `GBOOK: end` and sent the netlog.
- **Still to look at:**
  - PCIe 4's `genpd_suspend_noirq` still takes 3.28 s.
  - ath12k's `pci_pm_resume_early` takes 0.54 s.
  - At 142.17 s qrtr logs `failed to announce new service` / `failed while handling packet from 7:-2`.
  - `suspend-wakeup` is empty: no wakeup source counted the wake, although the EC logs the RTC event.
- **What fixed it (inference):** retaining the PCIe PHY GDSCs is what changed between -9/-10 and -11.
  - It fits the lead above: a collapsed QMP PCIe PHY loses its configuration, which explains both 6a's dead kept
    link and PCIe 4's hung re-init.
  - One boot proves only that it can work. Repeat boots show whether it is reliable.
- **Console boot #11** booted -10's image again after -10's reset, before -11 was written. It reset the same way after
  the resume (143.47 s), and is recorded with -10.
- **Idle and suspend power** stay unmeasured. On the bench the battery reports charging: `power_now` -2.96 W before
  and -2.49 W after, sign convention not checked.

## Update: the -11 loose ends, read from the netlogs (2026-11-04, 5:20 AM ET)

Read offline from `2026-11-04-netlog.txt#6` to `#8` and the source at a804c57. No new boots.

- **PCIe 4's 3.1-3.3 s `genpd_suspend_noirq` is silent.** No kernel line falls between its `calling` and `returned`
  lines, and there is no `Timeout waiting for L2 entry!` warning, so the 10 ms L2 poll
  (`pcie-designware-host.c:1255-1268`) did not time out.
  - What runs after the poll: `dw_pcie_stop_link()`, then `qcom_pcie_host_deinit()` (`pcie-qcom.c:1453-1470`). That
    asserts PERST, powers the WCN7850 off through pwrctrl (`pci_pwrctrl_power_off_devices`), powers the PHY off and
    runs the SoC-specific deinit.
  - The WCN power sequencer has only millisecond delays (`pwrseq-qcom-wcn.c:146-149, 234-249`). Which step takes 3 s
    is not known.
  - Next: one suspend boot with dyndbg for `pcie-qcom.c`, `pci-pwrctrl*` and `pwrseq-qcom-wcn.c`. It costs about 3 s
    of entry time only, so it is not urgent.
- **ath12k's 0.54 s `pci_pm_resume_early` is the WCN7850 booting again.** pwrctrl powered the chip off at suspend
  (above), so on resume MHI powers it on (`mhi mhi0: Requested to power ON` at 141.67 s, `Wait for device to enter SBL
  or Mission mode`). ath12k then logs the chip and firmware again (142.20 s). That is expected with pwrctrl power-off
  (inference).
- **The qrtr line is resume-only.** `failed to announce new service` / `failed while handling packet from 7:-2` appears
  once in each resume boot (netlog #6, #7, #8) and in none of the five boots without suspend (#1 to #5). It comes at
  142.17 s, while the WCN firmware is coming back over MHI. It is `net/qrtr/ns.c:490-493`: the name service could not
  broadcast a new server announcement (`ctrl_cmd_new_server`). Wi-Fi relinked in 8 s each time, so it looks harmless
  (inference).
