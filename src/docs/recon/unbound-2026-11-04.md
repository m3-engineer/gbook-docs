# Every enabled DT node binds: bandwidth monitors, videocc, camcc (2026-11-04)

Attempt `2026-11-03-35` (written before midnight, booted 2026-11-04 about 12:00 AM ET): kernel `#18` (commit
`d41ab28`, `kernel/mica-sync.config`), the board DTS with [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-no-psci-domain-idle-states.md)
(no PSCI domain idle states), image `4c8699959d256a77`. One cold start (the EC reset itself at power-on:
`Reset cause: sysjump hard efs`). Sources: the netlog `dumps/firstboot/2026-11-04-netlog.txt` and the AP console log
(both gitignored), `attempt.sh compare` against -34 boot 3.

## Change

`kernel/mica-sync.config` builds in three drivers that arm64 defconfig has as modules (the initramfs loads none):
- `QCOM_ICC_BWMON`: the four bandwidth-monitor PMUs `pmu@24091000` (`qcom,sc7280-llcc-bwmon`) and `pmu@240b3400`,
  `@240b5400`, `@240b6400` (`qcom,sdm845-bwmon`).
- `SM_VIDEOCC_8550`: `clock-controller@aaf0000` (`qcom,x1e80100-videocc`).
- `CLK_X1E80100_CAMCC`: `clock-controller@ade0000`.

The symbols come from `scripts/dt-kconfig-map.py` at `a804c57`; their four lines left `kernel/dt-gaps.txt` in the
same commit.

## Result

- **`unbound=` went from 6 to 0.** No line in dmesg says `sync_state() pending due to`, so every interconnect and
  clock provider reached `sync_state()`. `deferred=` is empty.
- No probe error from the new drivers (dmesg has no failure line naming bwmon, videocc or camcc).
- SCMI: 78 replies, max 3.9 ms, 0 timeouts. That is the fourth stall-free cold boot with no domain idle states. cpufreq
  is on all three clusters (710 MHz to 3.42 GHz).
- The touchscreen still fails (`hid-generic ... -22`). Its rail and enable pin read on this time (`ts-vreg=enabled
  users=1`, `gpio88 out high`, against disabled/low on -34 boot 3), which is the same failure with a different
  probe order (inference).
- `compare` labels this boot "warm": it took the AP reset at EC 179 s, which was `/init`'s reboot after the run, while
  the EC's own reset at power-on restarted its clock. The boot was cold.
