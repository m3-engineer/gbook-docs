## What the boots proved about the mapping

- **The SoundWire layout:** the four WSA8845 enumerate as `sdw:1:0:0217:0204:00:0` and `:1` (link 1 = `swr0`, unique
  ids 0 and 1) and `sdw:4:0:0217:0204:00:0` and `:1` (link 4 = `swr3`), as the stock codecs `wsa884x@02170220/1` under
  `wsa_swr_master` and `wsa2_swr_master` said. The LPI pins `wsa_swr_active` (gpio10/11) and `wsa2_swr_active`
  (gpio15/16) carry the buses.
- **The shutdown pins** TLMM 204 (bus 0's pair) and 205 (bus 3's pair) are the codecs' resets: the codecs bound once the
  reset chain had its drivers, and the stock overlay's pin groups are the same pins.
- **Supplies:** `vdd-1p8` = L15B as in stock; `vdd-io` = L12B (every other x1e80100 board, not in stock): accepted
  by the probes, still unverified as the right rail.
- **DMICs and the mic rail:** the VA macro bound with `dmic01_default`/`dmic23_default` and `vdd-micb-supply =
  <&vreg_dmic_3p3>` (the stock overlay's `EN_PP3300_MIC` on TLMM 69); capture device 3 exists; no capture test ran.

## The two blockers, both kernel options

At `a804c57` the WSA884x driver asks the reset framework first (`sound/soc/codecs/wsa884x.c:2022`,
`devm_reset_control_get_optional_shared`); the reset core turns a `reset-gpios` property into an auxiliary `reset-gpio`
device (`drivers/reset/core.c:1012-1105`), one per distinct pin, and defers the consumer until that device's driver binds.
That driver is `CONFIG_RESET_GPIO`, `=m` in the arm64 defconfig. With it built in (kernel #31) the `reset-gpio` devices
deferred themselves: TLMM 204 and 205 are each referenced by two codecs, and this kernel's shared-GPIO support
(`CONFIG_GPIO_SHARED=y`, `drivers/gpio/gpiolib-shared.c`) flags such a pin `GPIOD_FLAG_SHARED` and routes every request
for it through a `gpio-shared-proxy` platform lookup (`drivers/gpio/gpiolib.c:4805-4834`), whose chip only the
`CONFIG_GPIO_SHARED_PROXY` driver registers (`=m` by default). Both are `=y` in `kernel/mica-audio.config` since kernel #32.
A distro kernel loads both as modules, which is why the Yoga Slim 7x never showed this.

Reading: on each bus the hardware auto-enumerator numbers only the unique-id-1 amplifier; the other amplifier stays at
device number 0 (`AUTO_ENUM_FAILED`, bus clashes), so the DT's `speaker@0,0` slaves never attach and get no port setup.

loud, one soft, no left/right difference. The left pair (stock `@02170220`, unique id 0) never enumerates. Why is open:
both amplifiers on a bus share one reset pin (as in stock), the same rails, the same bus; candidates are an id-0 amplifier
that answers the enumeration read late or not at all, two amplifiers strapped to the same unique id (a clash on the id
read), or a reset/timing order the stock driver handles and mainline's does not. Sound then stops altogether after some
minutes: the re-enumeration after each bus clock-stop fails the same way, and reads of the id-1 slaves fail too
(`FIFO underflow`), inference from the debug lines at 1148 and 1260 s. `sdw status UNATTACHED` for the id-1 slaves while
they played is unexplained (the sysfs attribute may lag the hardware enumeration; unverified).

hardware enumerator, which mainline's does not ([asoc-debug-tools.md](../research/asoc-debug-tools.md), "The downstream driver").
