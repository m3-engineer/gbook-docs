Attempt `2026-11-02-3`: the first-boot image plus

- **SuperSpeed does not come up on Type-C yet:** the same stick runs at 5 Gbps on the build host. *Inference:* the PS8830
  retimer and the QMP combo PHY get no orientation or mode, because `pmic-glink` stays deferred
  (`qcom_pmic_glink: failed to initialize pdr`): it needs the ADSP running with its firmware.

## What changed from the first boot

The first boot left every dwc3 deferred ("failed to register DWC3 Core") because the eUSB2 and QMP PHYs, the eUSB2
repeaters, the PTN3222 redrivers, GENI I2C, TYPEC and PS883X were all `=m` in `defconfig` and no module loads. The
QMP children also need their menu parent `PHY_QCOM_QMP=y` (`drivers/phy/qualcomm/Kconfig:53-125` at `a804c57`);
`scripts/build-kernel.sh` caught that demotion.

## Attempt 2026-11-02-4: ADSP (failed, cause found)

`kernel/mica-adsp.config` + the stock `adsp.mdt` firmware in the initramfs. Results (AP console):
- `remoteproc0: Booting fw image qcom/x1e80100/google/mica/adsp.mdt`, then
  `qcom_q6v5_pas 6800000.remoteproc: Error in getting resource table: -5`; `adsp` stays `offline`.
- `pmic-glink` now probes (no longer deferred): the PD mapper and QRTR are built in. `qcom-battmgr-{ac,bat,usb,wls}`
  register but read empty, since the ADSP is down. No Type-C ports yet. The stick is still at 480M.
- The GPU SMMU and the DP aux bridges are no longer on the deferred list; SCMI and PCIe still are.

**Cause:** the resource-table request runs only when the PAS node has `iommus` (`qcom_pas_parse_firmware`, a804c57),
which mainline's `x1-el2.dtso` adds for the 9345. Mica's TrustZone answers `-EIO`. The stock mica tree has no `iommus`
on the ADSP or CDSP node (`entry00-base.dts:5513`, `:12925`) and uses the same `adsp.mdt`/`adsp_dtb.mdt` names.
**Fix:** `dts/mica-el2.fixups` deletes both properties after the overlay (attempt 2026-11-02-5).

## Attempt 2026-11-02-5: ADSP running, SuperSpeed on Type-C

Same kernel and firmware as attempt 4; the DTB drops the PAS `iommus` (`dts/mica-el2.fixups`).
- `remoteproc remoteproc0: remote processor adsp is now up` at 1.6 s; `adsp running`. `cdsp` stays offline (no
  firmware shipped: `-2`).
- `pmic_glink` UCSI registers **two Type-C ports**: `port0` (host, sink) and `port1` (host, source: powering the
  stick), both `orientation=normal`.
- `qcom-battmgr` answers: USB supply online, battery `Charging` (*inference:* power from the SuzyQ link in the left
  port); capacity still empty at capture time. UCSI adds two `ucsi-source-psy` supplies.
- **The kernel image stick enumerates on bus 7, the SuperSpeed hub of `usb_1_ss1`, at 5000M** (480M before).
- Still deferred: `arm-scmi` (mailbox) and both PCIe controllers.

USB on mica now works end to end: all four controllers, both Type-C ports at SuperSpeed (the right port tested),
Type-C role/orientation and battery reporting through the ADSP.
