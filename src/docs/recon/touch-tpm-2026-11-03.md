# Phase 3: touchpad, touchscreen and the Ti50 TPM (2026-11-03)

Attempts `2026-11-03-15` (image `b5f533fb178c67a2`, kernel `#13`, commit `7bffb22`) and `2026-11-03-16` (image
`bf2dd269aa121412`, same kernel, `/init` from commit `a9d7aeb`). Owner pick after the retro (2026-11-03): touchpad,
touchscreen and TPM. Sources: the netlog `dumps/firstboot/2026-11-03-netlog.txt` and the AP and EC console logs
`dumps/firstboot/2026-11-03-{ap,ec}.log` (all gitignored), and the pinned tree `a804c57`.

## Why

The retro's gap check (`scripts/dt-kconfig-map.py --gaps`, commit `4e1f825`) listed
`hid-over-i2c  I2C_HID_OF=m  touchscreen@10,touchpad@2c` and `google,cr50  TCG_TIS_I2C_CR50=m  tpm@50`. Both drivers
are `=m` in arm64 defconfig and the initramfs loads no modules, so none of the three nodes had ever bound.

## Changes (commit `7bffb22`)

- `kernel/mica-touch.config`: `I2C_HID_OF`, `I2C_HID_CORE`, `HID_MULTITOUCH` built in.
- `kernel/mica-tpm.config`: `TCG_TIS_I2C_CR50` built in; `TCG_TPM2_HMAC` forbidden at `=y` (it creates a primary key
  in the null hierarchy at every boot, `tpm2-sessions.c:1408-1412`). TPM traffic is probe-time startup, self-test and
  capability reads, and `TPM2_Shutdown(CLEAR)` at reboot (`tpm-chip.c:272`).
- Both compatibles left `kernel/dt-gaps.txt`.
- `/init`: `touch-*` captures with a touch window, `tpm` and `hwrng` captures; from `b6b6662` and `a9d7aeb` the
  `hid-over-i2c` IRQ counts around the window and a 30 s window behind a banner.

## TPM: pass (attempt -15)

- `cr50_i2c 1-0050: ti50 OT TPM 2.0 (i2c 0x50 irq 177 id 0x5066)` at 2.80 s (the driver's Ti50 name,
  `tpm_tis_i2c_cr50.c:693-702`).
- `tpm=tpm0 tpmrm0 major=2 driver=cr50_i2c`; `hwrng` lists `tpm-rng-0` beside `smccc_trng` (current).

## Touchpad: pass (attempt -16)

- `hid-multitouch 0018:27C6:0D4F`: `Mouse` (`event0`) and `Touchpad` (`event1`) on `2-002c`, both boots.
- -15: 0 events, but the owner missed the 20 s prompt. -16: `touch-events=event1 bytes=373296 events=15554` in the
  30 s window; IRQ `msmgpio 3 Level hid-over-i2c` went 646 to 2888.

## Touchscreen: probes on one boot of two, reports never arrive whole

- -15: `hid-multitouch 0018:1FD2:5010` bound (`event2`, and `event3` named `UNKNOWN`, likely a vendor collection;
  inference); no touch in the missed window.
- -16: the descriptor read at probe came back garbled: `hid-generic 0018:FFA5:FFFF.0001: unexpected long global item`,
  `probe with driver hid-generic failed with error -22`. In the window the IRQ (`msmgpio 51`) still went 305 to 921,
  and each report failed `i2c_hid_of 0-0010: i2c_hid_get_input: incomplete report (64/106)`
  (`i2c-hid-core.c:570-573`: 64 is the buffer size from the bad descriptor, 106 the size the panel sent).
- So the panel answers and reports touches; the descriptor read at probe is unreliable (inference: power-on timing).
  The node matches stock (descriptor address 0, TLMM 51 level-low, L15B and the TLMM 88 3.3 V switch with
  `startup-delay-us = <150000>` and `regulator-boot-on`, overlay `entry01` fragments 69 and 75). The 9345's panel is a
  different one (descriptor address 1), so it gives no precedent.
- Candidate fix (not tried): `post-power-on-delay-ms` on `touchscreen@10` (`i2c-hid-of.c:58-59`, `:105`). Not in the
  stock tree, so it would be marked unverified (ADR 0004). Judge it over several boots: one good probe in two is the
  baseline.

## Update: touchscreen attempts -17 to -19 (2026-11-03, 7:32 PM ET)

| Attempt | Change | Touchscreen at probe | In the window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `-17` | `post-power-on-delay-ms = <150>` (commit `42047ed`) | ID right (`1FD2:5010`), report descriptor `usage index exceeded`, `-22` | IRQ 1 to 730, every report `incomplete (64/106)` |
| `-18` | same, plus `dyndbg` for `i2c-hid-core.c:944` and `:804` (`GBOOK_CMDLINE_EXTRA`, commit `bc705a1`) | see the dump below | no input device |
| `-19` | `i2c8` at 100 kHz (commit `2d4c257`) | no message at all: no dump, not deferred, not unbound | no IRQ line registered |

**The -18 dump** (`i2c_hid_of 0-0010: HID Descriptor:`):
`1e 00 00 01 a7 03 21 00 24 00 6a 00 25 00 6c 00 22 00 23 00 a5 ff ff ff ff ff ff ff ff ff`.
- The first 20 bytes are a valid HID-over-I2C descriptor: length 30, version 1.00, report descriptor `0x3a7` (935)
  bytes at register `0x21`, input register `0x24`, **`wMaxInputLength` `0x6a` = 106**, output `0x25`/108, command
  `0x22`, data `0x23`.
- From byte 20 the read returns `0xff`: vendor `a5 ff` instead of `d2 1f`, then `ff` to the end. The report descriptor
  read gives `05 0d 09 04 43` and then `0xff` (a valid one starts `05 0d 09 04 a1 01`).
- The touchpad's dump on `2-002c` (another bus) is clean: `... 2f 00 ... c6 27 4f 0d ...` (`27C6:0D4F`,
  `wMaxInputLength` 47).
- So the panel's descriptor is right and the read loses data partway: the line reads high (`0xff`) once the device
  stops driving it (inference). The "64" on -16/-17 was a corrupted `wMaxInputLength`. The 30-byte read uses the GENI
  FIFO path (DMA starts at 32 bytes, `i2c-qcom-geni.c:528`), so SE DMA is not the cause.

**-19 at 100 kHz** is inconclusive: the probe ran earlier than on any other boot (touchpad at 1.5 s against 3.2 to
5.4 s), and the one silent failure path in i2c-hid is `nothing at this address` (`i2c-hid-core.c:1055`, `dev_dbg`,
not enabled). Speed and timing are mixed.

**Reverted** (commits `9126648`, `6d66b05`): the 100 kHz bus and the power-on delay, neither shown to help; the DTS
traces to stock again. Three boots did not converge, so the touchscreen is parked.

**Leads for a later session** (inference, untested):
- Stock reaches the panel through Qualcomm's vendor GENI I2C driver at 400 kHz; mainline's GENI clock table and
  transfer handling differ. Compare the SCL timing values (`i2c-qcom-geni.c` clock table) with a stock-side read.
- Enable `i2c-hid-core.c:1055` and the GENI I2C error lines in the next touch boot, so a silent probe failure shows.
- The panel's own reset or report-enable line (TLMM 89, `dts/README.md`) and its timing against the first read.

## Update: the touchscreen on the wire, attempt -21 (2026-11-03, about 8 PM ET)

Attempt `2026-11-03-21` (kernel `#16`, commit `778abbf`) traced the `i2c` and `qcom_geni_i2c` events
(`kernel/mica-debug.config`, [debug-tools.md](../research/debug-tools.md)); the probe-time trace is in the netlog.
- **Descriptor read clean this time** (30 bytes, FIFO path): `… 22-00-23-00-d2-1f-10-50-30-50-00-00-00-00`
  (`1FD2:5010`, version `0x5030`).
- **Report descriptor** (935 bytes, DMA path): `05-0d-09-04` then `ff` to the end; hid-generic failed `-22`.
- **Input reports** (64-byte reads, the fallback buffer): every one starts `6a-00-10-03-01-48-36-1d-33` (length 106,
  report ID `0x10`), and about half turn to `ff` after 9 to 12 bytes.
- **No controller error**: `geni-err` is empty; the GENI status shows ordinary done interrupts (`m_stat=0x08000081`).
- **Bus setup identical on both touch buses**: `clk_freq=400000 clk_div=2 t_high=5 t_low=11 t_cycle=22`
  (`i2c-qcom-geni.c:188`, the 19.2 MHz table). By the driver's formula (`:180-182`) that is about 1.15 µs low and
  0.52 µs high, under the I2C Fast-mode minimums (1.3 µs, 0.6 µs); the comment says the firmware adds cycles the
  formula leaves out (`:176-178`), so the real timing is unknown (inference). The touchpad tolerates it.
- **Reading:** the panel answers and starts each reply correctly, then lets the data line float (`0xff`) at a varying
  byte, with no error the controller sees. Bus timing that this touch controller does not tolerate fits (inference);
  -19's 100 kHz boot was inconclusive and is worth repeating with the trace on.

## Update: 100 kHz with the trace, attempt -22 (2026-11-03, 8:14 PM ET)

`i2c8` at 100 kHz again (commit `20a1f8a`), with tracing and `dyndbg` on `i2c-hid-core.c:1055` and
`i2c-qcom-geni.c:235-306`. The bus came up at 100 kHz (`clk_div=7 t_high=10 t_low=12 t_cycle=26`), and the
touchscreen did not acknowledge its address: `geni_i2c a80000.i2c: NACK: target device unresponsive, check its
power/reset-ln` twice, then `i2c_hid_of 0-0010: nothing at this address: -6`. That is -19's silent failure.
So the panel answers at 400 kHz (and drops out mid-read) and not at all at 100 kHz: the slower bus made it worse, so
"too fast for the panel" does not fit (inference). Reverted (commit `6671f61`); the DTS traces to stock again.
**Parked.** Untested leads: the panel's own enable and reset sequencing before the first read (TLMM 89, the 3.3 V
switch on TLMM 88, `vreg_l15b`), compared with what stock does at boot; and the stock driver's I2C settings.

## Update: panel follower and a full stock comparison, attempt 2026-11-04-1 (2026-11-04, 12:15 AM ET)

Offline first: the touchscreen's DT matches stock line for line. `ts0_default` (`int-n` gpio51; `reset-n` gpio48
output low; report-enable gpio89 output low), the 3.3 V switch on gpio88 with a 150 ms startup delay, `vdd` L15B,
`IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_LOW`, `hid-descr-addr` 0 (stock `entry01-overlay.dts` fragment@68 and fragment@75). The bus pins
match as well: stock sets `bias-pull-up` plus `qcom,i2c_pull`, and mainline's `bias-pull-up = <2200>` selects the same
TLMM I2C strong pull (`pinctrl-msm.c:467`, `i2c_pull_bit = 13` in `pinctrl-x1e80100.c` at `a804c57`).

Attempt `2026-11-04-1` (kernel `#18`, `dts/diag/touch-panel-follower.dtsi`, commit `5b9ee4e`): `panel` on the
touchscreen node points at the eDP panel, so i2c-hid powers and probes it only after the panel is prepared, as the
ChromeOS in-cell boards do (`sc7180-trogdor-homestar.dtsi:119`). The owner touched the screen during the prompt.
- **The link works and changes nothing.** The panel came up at 3.95 s and the touchscreen probed after it, at 4.30 s.
  The 30-byte HID descriptor read cleanly. The 935-byte report descriptor read `05-0d-09-04-43` and then `0xff` to
  the end (`unknown main item tag 0x4`, `-22`).
- **The interrupt fires and the controller reports.** There were 1936 `msmgpio 51` interrupts on CPU 0 and 1940
  input reads (64 bytes each; the device announces 106). 1549 of them turn to `0xff` from byte 5 to 8, while the rest
  read cleanly (`6a-00-10-03-01-b6-47-cc-3a-c6-07-cc-04-00 …`). There was no controller error.
- Reading (inference): the controller starts every reply right and then stops driving SDA at a random byte. With the
  DT, pins and pull-ups as stock and the panel timing ruled out, what is left is the controller side: the GENI SCL
  timing (19.2 MHz table: t_low ≈ 1.15 µs, t_high ≈ 0.52 µs by the formula, under the Fast-mode minimums,
  `i2c-qcom-geni.c:174-188`) or clock stretching that the controller does not honour. Stock runs a vendor GENI
  driver whose timing cannot be read from the image. -22's 100 kHz NACK does not fit timing alone.
- The fragment stays until the owner decides (status: failed for the probe; the link itself is harmless).

## Update: in-spec GENI timing, attempt 2026-11-04-2 (2026-11-04, 12:26 AM ET)

`kernel/patches/0008` (kernel `#19`) set the GENI 400 kHz entry to `2/6/13/24`, and the trace confirms it on both
touch buses (`geni_i2c_bus_setup: a80000.i2c: … t_high=6 t_low=13 t_cycle=24`, the same on `b80000.i2c`). No panel
follower. **No change:** the touchscreen's HID descriptor read `FFFF:FFFF` this time (`-22`), 608 of 773 reads ended
in `0xff`, and there was one `NACK: target device unresponsive`. The touchpad (`27C6:0D4F`) and the TPM worked at the
new timing. SCL timing is ruled out (within the formula's view of it). **The touchscreen is parked again.** Every
setting readable from the stock tree matches; what is left is the vendor GENI driver's behaviour, or the panel
controller's own state. The patch goes (a failed diagnostic, ADR 0007), as does `dts/diag/touch-panel-follower.dtsi`.
