# Sources: where the evidence comes from, and how to read it

Compiled 2026-11-04, 1:59 PM ET from the research and recon notes, and from the 2026-11-04 session's lookups. This is an index:
each claim lives in the note that cites it (last column), and a new finding goes there, not here. Primary sources
only. News coverage of the Googlebook exists in [recon notes](../recon/), but it is never the authority for a claim
(CLAUDE.md, "Docs conventions").

## Keeping it current

- A new research or recon note that relies on a new kind of source adds one row here, in the same commit.
- A source that becomes blocked, or a new way round, is recorded in "How to read them", with the date.
